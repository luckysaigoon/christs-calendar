Common questions

  • Add the calendar widget: touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Christ's Calendar → Verse Calendar.
  • Add the Lock Screen widget: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the widget area → Christ's Calendar.
  • Change the wallpaper every day: open the app and tap “Change Wallpaper Automatically Every Day” for step-by-step instructions.
  • Subscription: free for 2 months, then $3.99/month. Cancel anytime in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore a subscription on a new device, tap “Restore Purchases” in the app.

常见问题

  • 添加日历小组件：长按主屏幕 → 编辑 → 添加小组件 → 基督日历 → 经文日历。
  • 添加锁屏小组件：长按锁屏 → 自定 → 锁定屏幕 → 点小组件区域 → 基督日历。
  • 每天自动换壁纸：打开 App，点“Change Wallpaper Automatically Every Day”，按步骤设置。
  • 订阅：前 2 个月免费，之后每月 $3.99。可随时在“设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅”中取消。换新手机后，在 App 里点“Restore Purchases”恢复订阅。
