Common questions

  • When does the verse change? Every 12 hours, at midnight and at noon (your local time). The app, the Home Screen widget and the Lock Screen widget all change at the same time.
  • Add the calendar widget: touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Christ's Calendar → Verse Calendar. Swipe to choose small, medium or large, then tap Add Widget.
  • Add the Lock Screen widget: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the widget area → Christ's Calendar.
  • See another verse: in the app, swipe the verse left for the next one or right to go back. Your widgets show the verse you picked until the next change at midnight or noon, then everything returns to the usual order. Tap “Back to Current Verse” to return right away.
  • Change the card color: tap one of the colored dots under the verse in the app. The widgets follow your iPhone's Light or Dark Mode.
  • A widget shows an old verse: open the app once. This refreshes all the widgets.
  • Language: the app is in English and Simplified Chinese. The verses are in English, from the King James Version.
  • Subscription: everything is free for 1 month after you download. After that, subscribe for $3.99/month to keep seeing the daily verse. Cancel anytime in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore a subscription on a new device, tap “Restore Purchases” in the app.

常见问题

  • 经文什么时候更换？每 12 小时一次，在当地时间的零点和中午。App、主屏小组件和锁屏小组件会同时更换。
  • 添加日历小组件：长按主屏幕 → 编辑 → 添加小组件 → 基督日历 → 经文日历。左右滑动选择小号、中号或大号，再点“添加小组件”。
  • 添加锁屏小组件：长按锁屏 → 自定 → 锁定屏幕 → 点小组件区域 → 基督日历。
  • 看其他经文：在 App 里向左滑动经文看下一条，向右滑动回到上一条。小组件会显示你选的经文，到下一次零点或中午更换时恢复正常顺序。点“回到当前经文”可以马上回到原来的经文。
  • 更换卡片颜色：在 App 里点经文下方的彩色圆点。小组件跟随 iPhone 的浅色或深色模式。
  • 小组件还显示旧的经文：打开一次 App，所有小组件就会刷新。
  • 语言：App 界面支持英文和简体中文，经文为英文（钦定本 KJV）。
  • 订阅：下载后前 1 个月所有功能免费。之后订阅每月 $3.99，即可继续每天看到经文。可随时在“设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅”中取消。换新手机后，在 App 的订阅页面点“恢复购买”即可恢复订阅。
